Straight answers.

Are these predictions?
No. CupIQ does not publish win probabilities, trophy odds or tips. It measures how teams actually play — xG, pressing, build-up, set-pieces — and computes advanced stats on top, so you can judge the matchups yourself.
Is CupIQ a betting product?
No. CupIQ is a sports analytics and data platform. We provide statistics and metrics, not wagers or betting advice. Any decision you make with the numbers is your own.
Where does the data come from?
Team stats from FBref (StatsBomb-powered) and strength ratings from World Football Elo (eloratings.net), processed into derived metrics. Pre-tournament values are modeled and firm up as real matches are played.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free gives you the core stats — Elo, xG, xGA, possession, form. Pro (Analyst or Tournament Pass) unlocks all 20+ advanced and computed metrics, per-team percentile profiles, head-to-head comparison and CSV export for all 48 teams.
How often does it update?
The dataset is re-pulled on a schedule, roughly every three hours, with a faster cadence on match days.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Analyst is monthly and cancellable in one click. The Tournament Pass is a single payment with no recurring charge.
