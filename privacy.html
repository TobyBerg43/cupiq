Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This policy explains what CupIQ ("we", "us"), operated by CupIQ (a sole proprietorship), collects and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Email address — only if you give it to us (for example, to receive updates or to access a paid plan).
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number; we receive limited details such as your email, country, and whether a payment succeeded.
  • Usage and device data — basic analytics such as pages viewed, approximate region, browser type, and referring link, collected to understand traffic and improve the product.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and secure the service and paid access.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud (via Stripe).
  • To send you updates you asked for, and to respond to support requests.
  • To understand and improve how the product is used.

3. Third parties we use

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery for cupiq.app.
  • Google Fonts — the site loads fonts from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address as part of that request.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Analytics 4 — to measure traffic, advertising performance, and conversions. These set cookies; see the consent note below.

Each third party processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

Essential storage may be used to remember preferences or paid access. We also use Meta Pixel and Google Analytics 4, which set cookies to measure traffic and advertising performance. Where required by law (for example in the EU or UK), we ask for your consent before these load, and you can decline non-essential cookies.

5. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, tax records for payments). You can ask us to delete data we are not legally required to keep.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@cupiq.app. We do not sell your personal data.

7. International transfers & security

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, with appropriate safeguards. We use reputable providers and reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

8. Changes & contact

We will post changes here with an updated date. Questions or requests: support@cupiq.app.
