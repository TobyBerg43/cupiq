Responsible Use

Last updated: June 9, 2026
CupIQ is an analytics product, not a gambling operator. We publish statistics and advanced metrics for information and entertainment. We do not take bets, accept wagers, or facilitate gambling, and nothing here is a betting tip or a promise of any result.

How to read the stats

Every number on CupIQ is a statistic, not a prediction. We measure how teams have actually played; we do not forecast results or publish win probabilities. A strong statistical profile is not a guarantee — football is decided on the day, and any single match can defy the numbers. Treat the stats as one informed input, not a guaranteed answer.

If you choose to bet

  • Only bet what you can afford to lose, and only where it is legal in your jurisdiction.
  • Set limits before you start, and never chase losses.
  • Betting is for adults only — 21+ where applicable.
  • The decision, and the outcome, are entirely your own. CupIQ is not responsible for any losses.

Getting help

If gambling stops being fun or starts causing harm, help is available and free:

  • United States: call or text 1-800-GAMBLER, or visit ncpgambling.org.
  • United Kingdom: GamCare (0808 8020 133).
  • Elsewhere: search for your national problem-gambling helpline, or speak to a doctor.
