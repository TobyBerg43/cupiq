# Product

## Register

brand

(The marketing site `index.html` is the brand surface and the front door where the "premium" judgment is made. The two dashboards — `dashboard.html` free, `pro-k9x3f7q2a8.html` Pro — are **product** register: they serve a task and follow the product rules below. Treat each surface in its own register.)

## Users

- **Fans** settling debates and following the tournament: want clear win/advance/trophy probabilities and a few sharp numbers, fast.
- **Analysts and content creators**: want the full 15-metric depth, sortable tables, methodology transparency, and exportable data to build their own models.
- **Bettors** hunting an edge: want calibrated probabilities and the "why" behind each number. Context: skimming on desktop and mobile during a 39-day event (June 11 – July 19, 2026), often on match days.

## Product Purpose

CupIQ turns a public football dataset (FBref/StatsBomb event data, ClubElo ratings, a local Monte Carlo model) into clear, calibrated probabilities and advanced metrics for all 48 World Cup 2026 teams. It exists to be the sharpest **analytics** product for the tournament — explicitly not a gambling operator. Success: a visitor trusts the numbers, understands how they were made, and upgrades to Pro for the full dataset.

## Brand Personality

Precise, transparent, expert, calm. The voice of a serious data desk (think a football analytics newsroom), not a hype-driven tipster. Three words: **precise, transparent, confident.** Emotional goal: the quiet authority of a professional terminal — the reader feels they're seeing the real numbers, explained.

## Anti-references

- Betting/tipster sites: neon, "GUARANTEED PICKS", "beat the bookies", flashing odds. Never.
- Generic AI-SaaS landing: gradient-text headline, glassmorphic cards everywhere, a tracked uppercase eyebrow above every section, identical icon-card grids, the hero-metric template.
- Broadcast-graphic loudness: oversized shouting display type, emoji, flag-emoji-as-letters.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the numbers, don't describe them.** Every claim is backed by real data on the surface (a leaderboard, a chart, a metric), not adjectives.
2. **Transparency is the product.** The model's inputs, method, calibration, and last-updated time are always visible. No black box.
3. **Precision over decoration.** Restraint, hairline structure, tabular numerals, meaningful color. Effects (blur, gradient) only when they carry information.
4. **Density done well.** Serve experts dense, accurate detail without clutter; hierarchy and rhythm make density legible.
5. **Honest framing.** Probabilities are model estimates, never guarantees. Analytics, not gambling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text/UI ≥ 3:1, verified against the dark canvas.
- Full keyboard operability for dashboard controls (search, sort, filters, team detail); visible focus rings.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: every transition has a reduced/instant alternative.
- Color is never the only signal: probability tiers carry a label/value, not just hue (color-blind safe).
- Flags carry `alt` = country name; data has text equivalents.
